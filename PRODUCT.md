# Hunaa — منصة المحتوى العربي بالذكاء الاصطناعي

## Product Purpose
A premium Arabic content platform with AI-powered recommendations, featuring movies, series, reels, influencer collaborations, and music tracks. Serves Arabic-speaking audiences who value culturally authentic, high-quality entertainment experiences.

## Users
- Arabic-speaking content consumers aged 16–45
- Genre enthusiasts: drama, comedy, music, educational content
- Influencer followers and music fans
- Premium subscribers seeking curated, AI-recommended content

## Brand Tone
- **Luxurious & Welcoming**: Gold and dark-theme elegance suggest premium access and exclusivity
- **Culturally Rooted**: Islamic/Arabian architectural motifs (arabesque patterns, arch doorways) ground identity in heritage
- **Modern & Intelligent**: AI-driven recommendations and sleek navigation signal contemporary sophistication
- **Immersive**: Hero imagery, atmospheric effects (ken-burns animation), and layered depth create cinematic presence

## Anti-references
- Generic streaming UI (flat, corporate)
- Overly Western design language (avoid translating UI metaphors without cultural consideration)
- Cold minimalism (the brand owns warmth and richness)
- Dated or kitsch orientalism (AI slop trap: clichéd gold-on-black crypto aesthetic)

## Strategic Principles
1. **RTL First**: Arabic layout is native, not adapted. Hierarchy, alignment, and gesture design respect the language
2. **Ornament as System**: Arabesque dividers, arch iconography, and geometric patterns aren't decoration—they're the visual grammar
3. **Gold as Signal**: Use gold sparingly and purposefully (primary actions, focus states, premium markers). Never decorative blur or gradient text
4. **Cinematography**: All hero imagery should evoke narrative and emotion, not generic stock. Lighting and composition matter
5. **Modular Yet Opulent**: Components are clean and functional, but spacing and layering add richness without clutter

## Register
**Product** — design serves the platform. The UI disappears when content shines; the brand frames it.
